Assembly evidence, checked 2026-10-19

Confirmed geometry and sequence
1. Parts: one XIAO ESP32S3, one Wio-SX1262 expansion board for ESP32S3, one broad Wi-Fi/Bluetooth FPC antenna, one narrow LoRa FPC antenna.
2. Attach the broad 2.4 GHz antenna to XIAO before stacking, because the upper board reduces access to the socket.
3. Attach the narrow LoRa antenna to Wio. The two antenna steps may be exchanged, but the user's Wi-Fi then LoRa order is sensible.
4. Wio is above XIAO. Both shield/component sides face up. Wio's mounting-hole and button end is at the XIAO USB-C end. The two tiny rectangular B2B connectors mate at the opposite end from USB-C. Wio's connector is on its underside, XIAO's on its top side.
5. With USB-C facing the viewer, XIAO Wi-Fi socket is rear-right and Wio LoRa socket rear-left. With USB-C at top in a plan view, XIAO socket is bottom-left, Wio socket bottom-right.
6. The XIAO two 1x7 male GPIO header rows point below its PCB in the user's physical kit. They do not connect the two boards. Wio side holes remain open. Do not draw the ESP32S3 version mating through those holes.
7. Keep both RF leads outside the board gap. Show a small B2B close-up or underside inset so the actual interface is unambiguous.

Manual recommendation
Four requested steps are sufficient: parts, Wi-Fi antenna, LoRa antenna, board stack. Add short final-state checkpoint on stacking panel: both plugs seated, B2B seated, cables outside. Use same orientation across parts and detail views, with clear rotation only if changing to underside. Keep assembly disconnected from USB power, and connect antennas before power.
