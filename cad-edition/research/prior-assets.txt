All four photos are in /Users/ykondras/vid. The official kit-photo.png also shows both seated plugs and cable directions.

Observed visual shape
The loose connectors have a circular mating cup beneath a formed metal shell. From above, the shell is a flattened rounded lid with shoulders at the crimped neck. The cable leaves the neck horizontally relative to the board, perpendicular to the mating axis. Both antenna plugs use the same visual family. Their shell color is gold in the photos, though gray is suitable in the monochrome manual. The exact commercial part number has not been established.
The seated plug covers the socket's center pin and circular receptacle. Some square socket base remains visible. Never leave the pin sticking through the cap.
rf-plug-final.png improves upon the stacked discs, but its broad square lid and bulky neck are still exaggerated compared with the actual kit.

Recommended small icon
Use a compact flat rounded cap, one thin lower rim and a short tapered neck. Omit all but one fine crimp line. Reuse the same part. When routing the cable, rotate the plug around the mating axis rather than flipping its top and bottom. The first cable segment must follow the neck axis, then curve smoothly. A representative plug is approximately 3 mm wide and 4 mm long including its neck, small beside a 17.8 mm board. Use the CAD receptacle to control scale.

Recommended zoom icon
Show a flat closed top plate, short skirt, circular mating opening underneath and two subtle neck crimp tabs. The top has no hole. Keep the plug parallel to the board with the cup centered over the socket. Use one downward arrow normal to the board plane. Do not show the underside facing the viewer while implying a straight downward mate onto an upward-facing socket. In the attached state, the cap hides the pin. Arrows should align to the cup center, not the neck.

Instruction discrepancy
Seeed's general getting-started prose suggests placing one edge first. Connector-manufacturer guidance favors matched axes and vertical mating. For the manual show a centered plug and gentle downward seating, avoiding a large tilt or levering motion.

Independent scene QA, 2026-10-19
Reviewed scene-0.png through scene-3.png on white. Proof images are in research/qa-white.
Material issue 1: scene-3 LoRa cap is perched above the socket with its center pin and entire receiving cup visible below. Wi-Fi also leaves a tall stack of receptacle rings. Seated caps should cover the pin and upper socket cup, leaving only the base or a short mating seam visible.
Material issue 2: scene-0 cables approach the underside of the neck, wrapping below the cap. Correct the last curve segment or flip the cap so each cable exits the actual neck tip tangentially, away from the cap body.
Board proportions, board identities, stack orientation and the main-view RF mating axes appear consistent with the references. Arrows were intentionally absent at this review stage and will be added in layout.
