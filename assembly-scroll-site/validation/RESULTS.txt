LIMITS AND REPRODUCTION

Chromium 153.0.8010.37 on macOS. Phone and touch checks are emulated.
Safari, Firefox and physical devices were not tested. Slow connections
may skip intermediate poses while catching up. The 48 MiB bitmap limit
excludes canvas, GPU and other browser memory. The loader also limits
allocated images to 12 and concurrent jobs to four.

Run the commands in README.txt, then scripts/record_validation.py.
clean-layout.json records layout checks. browser-validation.json covers
full traversal. continuous-scroll.json and adaptive-performance.json
record motion and loading checks. site-source.json fingerprints the runtime.
Historical baseline files are retained.
